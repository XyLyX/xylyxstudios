Short bio

XyLyX Studios creates original music, visuals, and media experiments. Its featured release, Peat by Pollution, turns a whisky tasting at India Gate into electro-funk satire.

Studio bio

XyLyX Studios brings sound, image, and storytelling together through original releases and audiovisual experiments. Peat by Pollution pairs a spoken intro and dance chorus with a story about Delhi smog giving a Speyside whisky an unexpected Islay finish. The accompanying Wired Globe visualizer explores how different parts of a track can drive motion and color.
